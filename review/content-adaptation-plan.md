
# Content Review and Plan for Educational Use

## 1. Planned Use in the Study Guide

The developed content will serve as a foundation for a structured Study Guide covering the eight sections of the Introduction to AI module. The guide will present concepts in a logical sequence, beginning with basic definitions and gradually moving toward practical applications and responsible AI use.

Each section will contain relevant explanations, key terminology, examples, and learning activities. Where appropriate, worked examples and answer explanations will be included to help learners understand how concepts can be applied.

The planned structure is as follows:

| Section | Planned Study Guide Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Introduction to AI | Definition of AI, a brief history, everyday examples, and the importance of AI in modern society. |
| 2. Basic AI Concepts | Explanations of data, algorithms, models, training, and predictions, supported by simple examples. |
| 3. Different Approaches to AI | An introduction to symbolic AI, connectionist AI, and behavior-based AI, including a comparison of their main characteristics. |
| 4. Generative AI | Explanations of text and image generation, large language models (LLMs), prompts, and effective prompt writing. |
| 5. Applications of AI | Examples of AI in education, healthcare, business, science, and creative activities. |
| 6. Limitations and Risks of AI | Explanations of AI errors, hallucinations, bias, privacy concerns, and reliability. |
| 7. Responsible Use of AI | Practical guidance on verifying information, protecting personal data, being transparent about AI use, maintaining academic integrity, and applying human judgment. |
| 8. Using What We Learned About AI | Review activities and practical scenarios that require learners to apply concepts from the earlier sections. |

The Study Guide will use clear headings, tables, diagrams, and examples to make the information easier to navigate. Practice questions and answer explanations will allow learners to assess their understanding and identify areas that need further study.

## 2. Planned Use in the PPT Slides

The developed content can be adapted into a PowerPoint presentation that highlights the most important concepts without overwhelming learners with long paragraphs. The slides will emphasize key ideas, visual explanations, comparisons, and practical examples.

The presentation will be organized around the module's learning objectives and will follow the same sequence as the Study Guide.

### Planned Visual and Presentation Elements

| Topic | Planned Slide Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction to AI | Everyday examples of AI, such as recommendation systems and virtual assistants. | Connect AI concepts to learners' daily experiences. |
| Basic AI Concepts | A flow diagram showing how data can be processed by an AI model to produce a prediction. | Explain the relationship between data, models, and predictions. |
| Different Approaches to AI | A comparison table describing symbolic, connectionist, and behavior-based approaches. | Help learners identify the differences between AI approaches. |
| Generative AI | Examples of text and image generation, alongside sample prompts and outputs. | Demonstrate how generative AI responds to user instructions. |
| Applications of AI | A visual collection of examples from education, healthcare, business, science, and creativity. | Show the variety of problems AI can help address. |
| Limitations and Risks | Examples of inaccurate answers, biased outputs, and potential privacy concerns. | Encourage learners to recognize AI's limitations. |
| Responsible Use of AI | A checklist for verifying information, protecting privacy, and using AI ethically. | Provide practical guidance for responsible AI use. |
| AI Practice | Short questions, scenarios, and review activities. | Reinforce understanding and encourage active participation. |

The slides will use concise text, readable fonts, consistent formatting, and relevant visuals. Diagrams will be included when they make a concept easier to understand, while examples and questions will encourage learners to participate rather than passively read the presentation.

## 3. Planned Use in the Video Tutorial

The developed content can also be adapted into short, focused video tutorials. Each video will concentrate on one concept or practical skill so that learners can follow the explanation at a manageable pace.

The videos will combine narration, on-screen text, diagrams, and demonstrations where appropriate. Rather than repeating the entire Study Guide, each tutorial will focus on explaining a concept or showing learners how to apply it.

### Proposed Video Tutorials

#### Video 1: Understanding Basic AI Concepts

This video will introduce data, algorithms, models, training, and predictions. A simple diagram and a practical example will demonstrate how data can be used to train a model that makes predictions.

**Learning outcome:** Learners should be able to explain the basic roles of data, algorithms, models, training, and predictions.

#### Video 2: Getting Started with Generative AI

This video will demonstrate how to write a prompt for a generative AI tool. The presenter will compare a vague prompt with a more specific prompt and explain how details such as context, instructions, and desired output can affect the response.

**Learning outcome:** Learners should be able to write a clearer prompt and evaluate whether the resulting response meets their needs.

#### Video 3: Recognizing AI Limitations

This video will present an example of an AI response containing inaccurate or unsupported information. The presenter will demonstrate how to identify claims that require verification and compare them with reliable sources.

**Learning outcome:** Learners should be able to recognize possible AI errors and explain why important information must be verified.

#### Video 4: Using AI Responsibly

This video will demonstrate practical steps for responsible AI use, including protecting personal information, checking generated content, acknowledging AI assistance when required, and applying independent judgment.

**Learning outcome:** Learners should be able to identify responsible and inappropriate ways of using AI in academic and everyday situations.

The videos will be designed for beginner learners and will avoid unnecessary technical jargon. Captions, readable on-screen text, and clear narration will be used to improve accessibility. Where a demonstration involves an AI tool, the presenter will explain that its outputs may vary and should not automatically be treated as correct.

## 4. Alignment Between the Learning Materials

The Study Guide, PPT Slides, and Video Tutorials will support the same learning objectives while serving different learning needs.

| Resource | Main Purpose | How It Supports Learning |
|---|---|---|
| Study Guide | Provide detailed explanations and reference material. | Allows learners to read at their own pace, review definitions, and complete practice questions. |
| PPT Slides | Summarize important ideas using visual explanations. | Helps learners recognize key concepts, compare ideas, and follow classroom explanations. |
| Video Tutorial | Demonstrate concepts and practical skills. | Allows learners to observe step-by-step processes and revisit explanations when necessary. |
