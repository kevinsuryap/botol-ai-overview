## Source and Attribution Rules

1. Cite sources near the claims they support, not only in this file.
2. Check that each source actually supports the wording used in the lesson.
3. Prefer primary research, official documentation, standards, and authoritative institutional publications for technical or historical claims.
4. Record publication dates where available and check that time-sensitive information is current.
5. Identify borrowed images, diagrams, tables, and quotations, following the applicable licence and attribution requirements.
6. Do not copy long passages from sources. Explain ideas in the team's own words and cite the original material.
7. Keep genuine AI-use records separately in accordance with the Shared Assignment Rules. A reference list does not replace AI-use documentation.
8. Ask another team member to review the sources and citations before the section is marked checked.
