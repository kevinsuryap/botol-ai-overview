# 01. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## 1. Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become an important part of modern life. People encounter AI when using smartphones, searching for information online, receiving music recommendations, and interacting with digital assistants.

AI is changing how people interact with technology and complete everyday tasks. It is used in education, communication, transportation, entertainment, and many other areas.

This section introduces the meaning of Artificial Intelligence, explores its history, identifies common examples of AI in daily life, and explains why AI has become important in modern society.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, learners should be able to:

1. Define Artificial Intelligence in their own words.
2. Identify important milestones in the history of AI.
3. Recognise examples of AI in everyday life.
4. Explain why AI is important in modern society.
5. Describe how AI has influenced the way people interact with technology.

### Prerequisite Knowledge

No previous knowledge of AI or programming is required. Learners only need basic computer literacy and familiarity with everyday technologies such as smartphones, websites, and digital applications.

---

## 2. What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science concerned with creating computer systems that can perform tasks commonly associated with human intelligence.

These tasks may include recognising objects, understanding language, solving problems, identifying patterns, and generating content.

AI is not a single application or device. It is a broad field of technology used to develop systems for different purposes.

For example, an AI-powered translation application can help a person understand a sentence written in another language. A digital assistant can respond to spoken requests, while an online shopping platform may recommend products that match a user's interests.

Although AI can perform tasks that appear intelligent, it does not necessarily think, understand, or experience the world in the same way humans do. Its abilities depend on the system and the task it was designed to perform.

### 2.1 Examples of AI Capabilities

| Capability | What It Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Recognising information | Identifying objects, sounds, or other features | A phone recognising a face |
| Understanding language | Processing written or spoken words | A digital assistant responding to a question |
| Making predictions | Estimating a likely result | Predicting traffic conditions |
| Providing recommendations | Suggesting relevant options | A music application recommending songs |
| Generating content | Producing new material | An AI tool creating an illustration |

These examples demonstrate how AI can be applied to different activities. The specific capabilities vary depending on the application.

## 3. A Brief History of Artificial Intelligence

AI did not develop overnight. It has evolved through decades of research, experimentation, and technological progress.

### 3.1 Important Milestones

| Year or Period | Milestone | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| 1950 | Alan Turing published a paper exploring whether machines could demonstrate intelligent behaviour. | Helped shape early discussions about machine intelligence. |
| 1956 | The Dartmouth summer research project took place. | Helped establish Artificial Intelligence as a recognised research field. |
| 1980s | Expert systems attracted significant research and commercial interest. | Demonstrated how computer systems could represent specialised knowledge and support certain decisions. |
| 1997 | IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov in a chess match. | Demonstrated the ability of a computer system to perform exceptionally well in a complex game. |
| 2010s | Advances in computing and AI research led to major improvements in image recognition, speech processing, and other tasks. | Expanded the range of practical AI applications. |
| 2020s | Generative AI applications became widely accessible to the public. | More people could use AI to create text, images, and other content. |

These milestones represent only a small part of AI's history. Many researchers and technological developments have contributed to its progress.

### 3.2 How Has AI Changed Over Time?

Early AI research explored whether computers could perform tasks that appeared to require human intelligence. Over time, researchers developed increasingly capable systems for specialised tasks.

As computing technology advanced and AI applications became more widely available, people began encountering AI in everyday products and services.

Today, AI is used not only by researchers and large organisations but also by students, teachers, businesses, and members of the public.

This development has made understanding AI increasingly relevant to everyday life.

## 4. AI in Everyday Life

Many people use AI without realising it. AI may operate behind the scenes in applications that people already use.

### 4.1 Common Examples

| Area | Everyday Application | How It Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Smartphones | Voice assistants | Helps users perform tasks through spoken requests |
| Education | AI study applications | Provides explanations and learning assistance |
| Entertainment | Music and video recommendations | Suggests content based on user interests |
| Communication | Translation applications | Helps people communicate across languages |
| Transportation | Traffic prediction applications | Helps users plan journeys |
| Shopping | Product recommendation systems | Suggests products that may interest customers |
| Photography | Camera enhancement features | Improves or adjusts photographs |

AI applications differ in their purpose and capabilities. Some operate automatically in the background, while others require users to provide a request.

### 4.2 Worked Example: AI in a Music Application

Imagine that a student regularly listens to pop music using a music-streaming application.

After some time, the application begins recommending new songs that the student may enjoy.

The process can be understood through the following steps:

**Step 1: The student listens to music.**

The application records relevant activity according to its settings and privacy practices.

**Step 2: The application identifies patterns.**

It may identify similarities between the student's listening activity and other music preferences.

**Step 3: The application selects recommendations.**

The system chooses songs that it estimates may be relevant to the student.

**Step 4: The student receives suggestions.**

The recommended songs appear in a playlist or on the application's home page.

**Step 5: The student responds to the recommendations.**

The student's future activity may help the application adjust its suggestions.

This example illustrates how AI can influence an everyday digital experience. The recommendations are not always perfect, but they may help users discover music more easily.

### What Can We Learn from This Example?

- AI is already present in many familiar applications.
- AI can make digital services more personalised.
- AI can influence the options presented to users.
- Understanding AI helps people recognise how technology affects their daily activities.

## 5. Why Is AI Important?

AI has become important because it can support people in many areas of life and work.

### 5.1 Supporting Everyday Tasks

AI can help people complete certain tasks more conveniently.

For example, voice assistants can set reminders, translation applications can help users understand foreign-language text, and navigation applications can suggest routes.

### 5.2 Changing Education

AI is changing how some students access information and practise new skills.

Students can use AI-based educational tools to explore unfamiliar subjects, receive additional explanations, and generate practice questions.

These tools can provide extra learning support, although they do not replace the value of teachers, textbooks, independent study, or discussion with classmates.

### 5.3 Supporting Businesses and Industries

Organisations use AI for various activities, such as analysing information, assisting customers, and improving certain work processes.

For example, a company may use a digital assistant to respond to common customer questions, allowing employees to focus on more complicated requests.

### 5.4 Encouraging New Ideas

AI has created new possibilities in areas such as design, entertainment, research, and creative work.

Artists, musicians, writers, and designers may use AI tools to explore ideas or experiment with different approaches.

AI can support creative work, while people continue to make decisions about the meaning, quality, and suitability of the final result.

### 5.5 Preparing for the Future

As AI becomes more common, understanding the technology can help people make informed decisions about its use.

Basic AI knowledge is useful not only for people pursuing careers in technology but also for students and professionals in many other fields.

The aim is to understand what AI can do, recognise its limitations, and decide when its use is appropriate.

## 6. Learner Activity: Spot AI in Your Daily Life

### Objective

Identify everyday situations where AI may be used and explain how it helps people.

### Instructions

Read each situation below. Decide whether it likely involves AI based on the description.

| No. | Situation | Does It Likely Use AI? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A music application recommends songs based on listening activity. | Yes / No |
| 2 | A basic calculator adds two numbers using a fixed arithmetic operation. | Yes / No |
| 3 | A translation application uses an AI system to translate sentences. | Yes / No |
| 4 | A digital clock displays the time using its programmed functions. | Yes / No |
| 5 | A smartphone assistant responds to spoken questions. | Yes / No |
| 6 | An AI image application creates an illustration from a written description. | Yes / No |

### Discussion Questions

After completing the table, discuss the following questions:

1. Which of these applications have you used before?
2. Which example surprised you the most?
3. What other AI applications have you encountered?
4. How has AI made any part of your daily life easier?
5. Why is it useful to understand where AI is used?

### Answer Key

| No. | Answer | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yes | Recommendation systems may use AI to suggest relevant music. |
| 2 | Usually no | A basic calculator can perform arithmetic using fixed instructions without AI. |
| 3 | Yes | The situation explicitly describes an AI translation system. |
| 4 | Usually no | A basic digital clock does not need AI to display the time. |
| 5 | Yes | Modern voice assistants commonly use AI to process spoken requests and respond. |
| 6 | Yes | The application uses AI to generate an image from a written description. |

**Note:** These answers depend on the specific application described. Some calculators and clocks may include additional AI-powered features, but their basic functions do not require AI.

### Expected Learning Outcome

Learners should be able to recognise common AI applications and explain how AI is used in familiar situations.

## 8. Key Takeaways

- **Definition:** Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science focused on systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence.
- **History:** AI has developed over several decades through research and advances in technology.
- **Everyday use:** AI appears in applications involving communication, education, entertainment, transportation, shopping, and other activities.
- **Importance:** AI can support everyday tasks, education, businesses, and creative work.
- **Future relevance:** Understanding AI helps people recognise how technology influences society and make informed decisions about its use.

AI is no longer limited to laboratories or specialised computer systems. It has become part of many everyday experiences.

Understanding what AI is and where it is used provides a foundation for learning more about the technology in the following sections of this module.
