# Lesson 2: Understanding AI Approaches

## 1. Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems use different approaches to solve problems, make decisions, recognise patterns, and generate content. The approach used depends on the task, the available information, and the intended outcome.

Some systems follow predefined rules, while others learn from data or generate new content based on instructions. Understanding these approaches helps learners recognise how different AI applications work and why certain methods are more suitable for particular tasks.

This lesson introduces four common AI approaches: rule-based systems, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI.

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, learners should be able to:

- Define the term *AI approach*.
- Identify four common AI approaches.
- Explain the main differences between these approaches.
- Match AI approaches to suitable real-world applications.
- Identify the strengths and limitations of each approach.

### Prerequisites

Learners should have a basic understanding of what Artificial Intelligence is and how it is used in everyday life.

## 2. What Are AI Approaches?

AI approaches are methods used to design systems that perform tasks associated with intelligence, such as solving problems, recognising patterns, making predictions, or generating content.

Different approaches use different methods to achieve their goals.

For example, a rule-based system follows instructions written by developers, while a machine learning system uses examples to identify patterns and make predictions.

There is no single approach that is best for every situation. The most suitable approach depends on the problem, the available resources, and the expected results.

## 3. Common AI Approaches

### A. Rule-Based Approach

A rule-based approach uses predefined rules and logical instructions to make decisions. Developers establish the rules before the system is used, and the system follows those rules when responding to specific situations.

**Example:** A school website displays a password-reset guide whenever a user selects "Forgot Password."

**Advantages:**

- Easy to understand and predict.
- Suitable for tasks with clear conditions.
- Relatively simple to maintain when the rules are limited.

**Limitations:**

- Cannot easily handle situations that are not covered by its rules.
- Requires developers to update the rules when requirements change.
- May become complicated when many rules interact.

### B. Machine Learning Approach

Machine learning is an AI approach in which systems learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions. Instead of requiring developers to write every decision rule manually, the system learns from examples during training.

**Example:** An email service learns from labelled examples of spam and legitimate messages to classify incoming emails.

**Advantages:**

- Can recognise patterns that may be difficult to describe with fixed rules.
- Can make predictions about new examples.
- Can be improved by training with suitable data and evaluating its performance.

**Limitations:**

- Depends on the quality and relevance of the training data.
- May make incorrect predictions.
- Can reproduce biases present in its training data.

### C. Deep Learning Approach

Deep learning is a specialised type of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns. It is especially useful for tasks involving images, speech, audio, and language.

**Example:** A photo application uses a deep learning model to recognise objects such as cars, trees, and animals in images.

**Advantages:**

- Can learn complex patterns from data.
- Supports many advanced image and speech recognition applications.
- Can work effectively with large and complex datasets when properly trained.

**Limitations:**

- Often requires substantial computing resources.
- May require large amounts of training data.
- Its predictions can be difficult to explain.

### D. Generative AI Approach

Generative AI refers to AI systems designed to create new content based on patterns learned from existing data. Depending on the model, the content may include text, images, music, audio, or computer code.

**Example:** A student uses an AI assistant to create a first draft of a presentation outline.

**Advantages:**

- Supports brainstorming and creative work.
- Can produce different types of content.
- Helps users develop ideas and prepare initial drafts.

**Limitations:**

- May produce false or misleading information.
- Generated content may contain errors or bias.
- Requires human review, especially when the information is important.

**Important distinction:** These approaches are related, not completely separate categories. Deep learning is a type of machine learning, and many generative AI systems use deep learning. A single application may combine several approaches.

## 4. Comparing AI Approaches

| Approach | How It Works | Example Application | Main Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule-based | Follows predefined rules | Calculating delivery fees | Limited flexibility |
| Machine learning | Learns patterns from data | Email spam detection | Depends on data quality |
| Deep learning | Uses neural networks with multiple layers | Image recognition | Often requires substantial computing resources |
| Generative AI | Creates content using learned patterns | Generating a story or presentation draft | May produce inaccurate content |

The main difference is how each approach handles a task. Rule-based systems rely on predefined instructions, machine learning learns patterns from examples, deep learning learns complex patterns using multilayer neural networks, and generative AI focuses on creating new content.

However, the categories can overlap. For example, a generative AI assistant may use a deep learning model, while a larger application may combine a machine learning classifier with a rule-based decision system.

## 5. How to Choose an AI Approach

Choosing an AI approach begins with understanding the problem that needs to be solved.

Consider the following questions:

1. **What is the task?** Is the system following rules, recognising patterns, or creating content?
2. **What information is available?** Does the system need training data, or are predefined rules sufficient?
3. **How accurate must the result be?** Some tasks require highly reliable and predictable outputs.
4. **What resources are available?** More complex models may require additional computing power and development time.
5. **What risks must be considered?** Privacy, bias, reliability, and human oversight may affect the choice.

For tasks with clear conditions, a rule-based system may be sufficient. Machine learning may be useful when a system needs to identify patterns in examples. Deep learning can be appropriate for complex data such as images and audio, while generative AI is useful when the goal is to create new content.

The best choice depends on the specific requirements rather than the popularity of a particular technology.

## 6. Practical Scenario: Improving a School Support System

Imagine that a school wants to improve how it answers students' questions.

The school could use several AI approaches for different tasks:

1. **Rule-based system:** Displays a fixed answer when a student selects a common question, such as how to reset a school account password.
2. **Machine learning:** Classifies incoming enquiries into categories such as timetable, registration, or technical support.
3. **Deep learning:** Processes spoken enquiries or recognises text in uploaded documents, depending on the system's design.
4. **Generative AI:** Drafts a natural-language response to a student's question using relevant information provided to the system.

Each approach serves a different purpose. Rule-based systems provide predictable responses, machine learning helps classify enquiries, deep learning can process complex inputs, and generative AI can draft responses.

In practice, the school might combine these approaches rather than use only one. Staff should review important responses, and sensitive student information should be handled appropriately.

1. A delivery website calculates shipping fees using fixed conditions based on distance and package weight.

3. A computer system analyses photographs to recognise different types of animals.
4. A writing assistant creates a short story based on a user's instructions.
5. A school system places incoming enquiries into categories by learning from previously labelled examples.

For each answer, explain why the selected approach is suitable.

## 9. Discussion and Reflection

Discuss the following questions individually or in groups:

1. Why do AI systems use different approaches instead of relying on one method?
2. What is the main difference between a rule-based system and a machine learning system?
3. Why can the quality of training data affect machine learning results?
4. What are the benefits and limitations of using generative AI for schoolwork?
5. Why should people evaluate AI-generated results rather than automatically trust them?
6. Can you think of a situation in which combining two AI approaches would be more useful than using only one?

## 10. Key Takeaways

- **Rule-based systems** follow predefined instructions and conditions.
- **Machine learning** identifies patterns in data to make predictions or decisions.
- **Deep learning** uses multilayer neural networks to learn complex patterns and is a specialised form of machine learning.
- **Generative AI** creates new content based on patterns learned during training.
- AI approaches have different strengths and limitations, and several approaches can be combined in one application.
- Choosing an appropriate approach requires considering the task, available data, resources, reliability, and potential risks.

Understanding AI approaches helps learners make more informed decisions about how AI applications work and when different methods may be useful.
