# Section 7: Responsible AI Use

## 7.1 Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) can help people learn, solve problems, generate ideas, and complete tasks. However, AI systems can also produce incorrect information, reflect bias, or create privacy risks. Therefore, people should understand how to use AI responsibly.

Responsible AI use means using AI in ways that are honest, safe, fair, and respectful of others. AI should support human thinking and decision-making rather than replace human judgment completely.

## 7.2 Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, learners should be able to:

1. Explain what responsible AI use means.
2. Identify possible risks when using AI-generated information.
3. Describe ways to check the accuracy of AI-generated content.
4. Explain why transparency and academic honesty matter.
5. Identify privacy concerns when sharing information with AI tools.
6. Apply responsible AI principles to everyday and educational situations.

## 7.3 Prerequisites

Before studying this section, learners should understand:

- Basic AI concepts.
- What generative AI is and how people use it.
- That AI-generated content may contain errors or bias.

## 7.4 Key Question

**How can we benefit from AI while reducing the risks it may create?**

## 7.5 What Is Responsible AI Use?

Responsible AI use involves making careful decisions when using AI tools. It includes checking information, protecting private data, respecting other people, and following relevant rules.

Important principles include:

- **Accuracy:** Check whether AI-generated information is correct.
- **Transparency:** Be honest about when and how AI was used.
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for the final work and decisions.
- **Privacy:** Avoid sharing personal or confidential information without permission.
- **Fairness:** Consider whether AI outputs may be biased or unfair.
- **Human judgment:** Review AI outputs instead of accepting them automatically.

These principles help people use AI as a tool while remaining responsible for their actions.

## 7.6 Checking AI-Generated Information

AI tools can generate answers that sound convincing but contain mistakes. They may provide outdated information, incorrect statistics, or references that do not support the claims being made.

To check AI-generated information:

1. Identify important claims, facts, statistics, and quotations.
2. Compare them with reliable sources, such as official websites, textbooks, academic publications, or trusted organizations.
3. Open and read the original sources rather than relying only on AI summaries.
4. Check whether the sources actually support the claims.
5. Correct or remove information that cannot be verified.

### Example

A student asks an AI tool to explain climate change. The AI provides a statistic about global temperatures but does not identify a reliable source.

The student should find a trustworthy scientific source, check the statistic, and revise the explanation if necessary.

**Key point:** A confident answer is not necessarily a correct answer.

## 7.7 Being Honest and Transparent

Transparency means explaining how AI contributed to a task when disclosure is required or appropriate.

For school assignments, students should follow their teacher's instructions and school policies about AI use. Some assignments may allow AI for brainstorming but prohibit AI-generated final answers.

Responsible practices include:

- Following the rules provided by the teacher.
- Acknowledging AI assistance when required.
- Distinguishing personal ideas from AI-generated suggestions.
- Checking and editing AI-generated content before submitting work.
- Keeping a record of how AI was used when appropriate.

Students should not claim that AI-generated work is entirely their own if this misrepresents their contribution.

### Example

A student uses AI to brainstorm ideas for a presentation. The student then researches the topic, selects the strongest ideas, writes the presentation in their own words, and discloses the AI assistance if required.

This approach uses AI as a support tool while preserving the student's responsibility for the final work.

## 7.8 Why Human Judgment Matters

AI can assist with analysis and decision-making, but it does not guarantee that its recommendations are correct, fair, or suitable for every situation.

Human judgment is important because people can consider context, values, consequences, and information that an AI system may overlook.

For example, an AI tool may recommend a student study plan based on the information it receives. The student should still consider their own learning needs, schedule, and teacher's advice before following the recommendation.

People should be especially careful when AI is used in situations involving health, safety, finances, education, or other important decisions.

**Remember:** AI can provide suggestions, but people remain responsible for how they use those suggestions.

## 7.9 Using AI for Schoolwork

AI can support learning when it is used appropriately. It can explain difficult concepts, provide practice questions, suggest essay structures, and give feedback on drafts.

However, using AI without understanding the material can reduce learning and may violate academic rules.

### Appropriate uses

- Asking AI to explain a difficult concept in simpler language.
- Generating practice questions for revision.
- Requesting feedback on grammar or organization.
- Brainstorming possible research questions.
- Comparing explanations from AI with reliable learning materials.

### Uses that require caution

- Copying AI-generated answers without understanding them.
- Submitting AI-generated work when the assignment prohibits it.
- Using invented or unverified references.
- Allowing AI to complete every part of an assignment without meaningful personal contribution.
- Sharing another student's work or private information without permission.

The correct use of AI depends on the purpose of the assignment and the rules set by the school or teacher.

## 7.10 Protecting Personal and Confidential Information

AI tools may process information entered by users. Depending on the tool and its settings, submitted information may be stored or used in different ways.

Users should therefore think carefully before sharing information with an AI system.

Avoid entering sensitive information, such as:

- Passwords or login credentials.
- Personal identification numbers.
- Private contact details.
- Confidential school or workplace documents.
- Another person's private messages or personal information without permission.

Before using an AI tool, review its privacy settings and applicable rules. When possible, remove identifying details from examples and documents.

### Example

A student wants AI to summarize class notes. The notes contain a classmate's phone number and private messages.

The student should remove the unrelated personal information before using the tool and should not share another person's private information without permission.

## 7.11 Fairness, Bias, and Potential Harm

AI systems can produce biased or unfair results. This may happen because of limitations in their training data, design, or the way people use them.

For example, an AI system used to recommend job applicants might unfairly favor certain groups if its data or decision process contains bias.

When reviewing AI outputs, users should ask:

- Does the answer make unfair assumptions about a person or group?
- Is important information missing?
- Could the result disadvantage someone?
- Is the information appropriate for the situation?
- Should a person with relevant knowledge review the result?

AI outputs should be evaluated carefully, especially when they may affect people's opportunities, rights, or well-being.

## 7.12 Responsible AI Use Checklist

Before using or submitting AI-generated content, ask yourself:

- [ ] Have I checked important facts using reliable sources?
- [ ] Have I followed the relevant rules and instructions?
- [ ] Have I acknowledged AI assistance when required?
- [ ] Have I avoided sharing private or confidential information?
- [ ] Have I considered whether the output may be biased or unfair?
- [ ] Do I understand and agree with the final content?
- [ ] Have I taken responsibility for the final result?

If you cannot answer "yes" to an important question, review your work before proceeding.

## 7.13 Learner Activity

### Scenario

A student is preparing a report about renewable energy. The student uses an AI tool to generate most of the report.

The student notices that some statistics have no clear sources but includes them without checking. The student also uploads a classmate's private notes without permission and submits the report without acknowledging AI assistance, even though the teacher requires disclosure.

### Questions

1. Identify three problems with the student's use of AI.
2. Why should the student verify the statistics and references?
3. What should the student do before sharing the classmate's notes?
4. How can the student use AI while following academic honesty rules?
5. Name two ways the student could improve the report before submitting it.

### Expected Answers

1. The student uses unverified information, shares another person's private notes without permission, and fails to disclose AI assistance as required.
2. Verification helps ensure that the report contains accurate information and reliable evidence.
3. The student should obtain permission and remove private or unnecessary information. If permission is not granted, the student should not upload the notes.
4. The student should follow the teacher's rules, acknowledge AI assistance when required, check the content, and contribute their own understanding and work.
5. The student could verify statistics using reliable sources, correct unsupported claims, rewrite sections in their own words, and add the required disclosure.

*Other reasonable answers may be accepted if they demonstrate responsible AI use.*

## 7.14 Reflection

Think about an AI tool you have used or might use for learning.

Write a short response to these questions:

1. What task could AI help you with?
2. What information would you need to verify?
3. What personal information should you avoid sharing?
4. How would you make sure your use of AI follows the relevant rules?

## 7.15 Key Takeaways

- AI can be useful, but its outputs are not always accurate or fair.
- Important claims should be checked using reliable sources.
- Users should be honest about AI assistance when required.
- Personal and confidential information should be protected.
- AI should support learning and decision-making, not replace human responsibility.
- Responsible AI use requires critical thinking, fairness, transparency, and accountability.
